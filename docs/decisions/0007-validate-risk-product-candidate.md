# Decision 0007: Validate the risk-product candidate

Date: 2026-08-18

## Decision

Accept the local candidate as meeting the pre-registered portfolio risk-product threshold. The independent
Director of Risk review scores it 9.25 of 10, with no dimension below 1.65 of 2. Keep production readiness
explicitly unestablished.

## Why

The candidate combines strong model evidence with an executable refusal, temporary controls, rule
dispositions, monitoring triggers, an exit path, decision-changing interactions, and explicit evidence
boundaries. It clears the stated quality bar without manufacturing missing operating evidence.

## Alternatives rejected

- Increase the score after correcting review findings. The strict independent score already passes and is
  preserved unchanged.
- Call the candidate production-ready. The required operating outcomes, owners, and feeds do not exist.
- Publish immediately. Publication, remote reconciliation, and repository metadata remain human-gated.

## Not done

No push, deployment, repository metadata change, paid resource, visibility change, or public release was
performed.

## Changed

The scored review, release narrative, candidate manifest, and regression contracts now distinguish the
local candidate, the currently deployed release, carried-forward evidence, and the remaining publication
approval.
