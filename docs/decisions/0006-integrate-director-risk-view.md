# Decision 0006: Integrate the Director of Risk view

Date: 2026-08-18

## Decision

Add one visible ruled risk disposition beneath the failed checks and one native disclosure for the seven
operating controls. Put paired uncertainty beside the existing same-capacity comparison and put each rule
disposition beside its existing scenario control.

## Why

The previous workspace explained why promotion was refused but still made the reader assemble the interim
control, uncertainty, monitoring response, and exit path from separate technical records. A Director of
Risk needs that disposition in the decision flow.

## Alternatives rejected

- Add a new page, sidebar, or modal. The committee-exhibit structure already has the right hierarchy.
- Add another chart. The missing content is operating responsibility and evidence boundaries, not another
  visualization.
- Hide rejected rules. Keeping them as simulations shows why intuitive overrides worsen the queue.

## Not done

No scenario can change the refusal. No rule creates an applicant decision. No production control is marked
as passing when its data is unavailable.

## Changed

The stakeholder surface, public narrative, responsive browser assertions, and paired screenshots now carry
the current decision, temporary baseline, uncertainty, rule dispositions, monitoring triggers, and evidence
required to reopen governance.
